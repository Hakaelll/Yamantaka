# Rust visual replacement

The primary audience is personal-library reading and study, confirmed 2026-10-08.
The user rejects the incumbent visual quality and asks for a striking professional
replacement. The functional-section requirement makes the executed library the
first validation surface. No comp-first workflow preference has been saved.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A gallery for a personal collection. Covers carry the visual identity;
catalog actions occupy two quiet horizontal bands, with no persistent sidebar.

OWN-WORLD: Neutral black and graphite, clear ivory lettering, precise violet focus
and primary actions; warm paper in light mode. Segoe UI Variable. Matte cover volumes,
spines and edge geometry; restrained border radii and generous catalogue gutters.

STORY: Recognize the collection, resume a book, find another, inspect it and read.
Context and errors remain explicit without replacing document content with chrome.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact utility row, a library title/search band, a continuity
row with the actual small cover, title and progress, then responsive cover volumes
with readable titles below. The continuity row disappears at small sizes and its
action moves to the utility row. List
view uses the same ordering and filters. The signature interaction is cover-only
pointer lift with separate selection and keyboard-focus treatments; reduced motion
keeps an equivalent static volume.

STAGE 2: The user's rejection of the first captures requires a substantial
composition and material revision. Controls use drawn vector icons and their own
focus, pressed and selection states. Covers keep rigid projected volume, six
procedural families, contact shadows, subdued foil and pointer-driven springs.
Segoe UI remains the interface face required by the brief. A locally bundled,
OFL-licensed Source Serif 4 Display face accompanies editorial document titles and
three jacket families; its provenance is in assets/fonts/source-serif. The reader
uses one compact toolbar and a centered paper surface. Settings uses four working
categories with selectable previews of the actual two themes. At 200% in 640x480,
controls reflow and the complete surface remains vertically scrollable.

FORM: Gallery study room, seventh grounded candidate, seed 26a7630f. The alternatives
were editorial catalogue, collection archive, reading desk, jacket display, study
index and print cabinet. The brief's palette and local typography constrain the
concept. The cloud, tensegrity, printmaking, pixel and detector directions lose
product clarity as literal UI grammars: keep their discipline of narrow accent,
structural alignment, tonal restraint, explicit states and legible data respectively,
without transferring their decorative forms.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish
review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

This is a development contract, not a claim of visual approval. Native captures,
interaction validation and physical accessibility checks remain acceptance gates.
